❤FUJIMINE❤

The Vanquisher Witch

Who is Fujimine?

Fujimine is an arc villain in the manga Ichi the Witch. She debuts in chapter 79, during the Magikeepers Arc, where she raids the Mantinel Witches Association all by herself with the goal of killing Chun, Jikishirone, Madoka, and Ichi. More importantly, Fujimine is the love of my life.

A sharp, playfully destructive witch with a kill count of over a thousand people, Fujimine is one of the Magikeepers' top bounties. In contrast with her remorseless criminal record, she is quite affable and somewhat affectionate. She gives people cutesy nicknames and loves teasing to get a reaction.

After defecting from the Mantinel Witches Association, Fujimine has allied herself with World-Hater Majik. That being said, she's unafraid of their prowess and does not hesitate to act against their order—even going so far as to suggest killing Ichi with them watching.

As of August 22nd, 2026, the latest chapter Fujimine has appeared in is 93 (as a flashback. Her actual most recent appearance chapter 92). Between her debut and her most recent chapter, Fujimine has made an appearance in every chapter except for chapters 83 and 90. To my knowledge, there are two coloring errors. In page 17 of chaper 79, her right hand is misdrawn and uncolored. This error was not corrected in the official volume 9 print. In the first page of chapter 80, her moles are missing. Volume 10 isn't out yet, so I'm not sure if this was corrected.

I think Fujimine is very cute and interesting. I became obsessed with her the moment I saw her, because she initially seemed very similar to one of my favorite OCs. However, as I learned more about Fujimine, I realized that they are actually very different in style, priorities, methods of evil, etc... Overall, Fujimine feels a lot more elegant/mature than my OC. I still enjoy seeing their similarities, though!

I get a little embarrassed to talk about it sometimes, but thinking about Fujimine makes me really happy. Unhinged flirty girls are often pretty hit or miss for me. Either I'm icked out by them, or I totally fall in love. Of course, this was a case of the latter. I look forward to every new chapter of Ichi the Witch with her in it—I don't know where Nishi will take her story, but I trust that it will make me happy no matter what!

Fujimine's CompHet - my embarrassing literary analysis

Fujimine is a lesbian. I know this to be true. Could it be me projecting? Perhaps. But I have a fair case to make here. So...hear me out.

Fujimine's lesbianism is somewhat complicated by her compulsory heterosexuality (comphet)—that is, the effects of a societal pressure for women to romantically be attracted to and pursue men. I believe that comphet is integral to her character and understanding her totally canon lesbianism. Let's break it down.

In chapter 80, Fujimine launches an unsanctioned attack on the Mantinel Witches Association, using Amadoro Ribboncandy as her figurehead aggressor. This is all done without World Hater Majik's permission (apparently they're leading/monitoring her...?)

To justify her own actions, Fujimine says the following:

"You know how it goes. When a girl has a boy on her mind, she can't help but tease him."

I've seen this line pointed at as proof of Fujimine's heterosexuality/attraction to men, however I believe that she is actually attempting to emulate a hegemonic ideal. In this world, Ichi the only male witch in all of human history, and thus he is a subject of attraction for many witches/women who like men (e.g. Spica, some background character witches from chapter 1, etc.). When Fujimine says "When a girl has a boy on her mind, she can't help but tease him," she's not speaking specifically for herself. What she's actually doing here is outlining a social expectation/rule—girls naturally tease and dote on boys. In her eyes, that's "how it goes," but that doesn't necessarily mean that this is the case for her.

As far as we know, Fujimine doesn't have genuine/intimate relationships with other people. From her introduction in chapter 79 and onwards, she's shown to be affectionate with others by giving out nicknames. With World Hater Majik, she tries to go out of her way to "help" them, even if they don't want help. With Hakari, she isn't afraid to lay across their lap and stroke their fur (and ego). Though, these aren't good reference points for friendship. Fujimine is only trying to curry favor with powerful majiks, and the majiks themselves don't see her as a friend (they actually quite outwardly express dislike or disgust).

Even among other humans, Fujimine's ability to connect with others is severely warped. In chapter 84, she addresses those she's "dosed with love" as her close friends. It's not entirely clear if she's fully aware that this is all pretend, or if she's so far gone that she believes that these relationships are genuine. When revealing to Chun that she had mind-controlled him at one point, she uses the same sort of language.

"All your precious friends died because you and I worked together with 'love.' Isn't that sweet?"

The blurred line between genuine and contrived relationships ties into another facet of Fujimine's character—her desire to separate herself from humanity. In chapter 87, Fujimine says something very interesting after Ichi stabs her in the neck.

"You, Ichi...aren't cut out to be human. If you ever get bored of playing at being one...we'd be happy to have you"

Contrary to what her musing in chapter 80 suggests, Fujimine's interest in Ichi is not one of romantic attraction. Fujimine has no intention of playing by human limitations or rules, and she sees Ichi as someone who effortless exists outside of these boundaries. Ichi is humanity's first male witch, and his death-for-death philosophy drives him to harm other humans without hesitation. Purging what makes her human is ultimately what fulfills Fujimine, and she assumes that Ichi is like her.

It's likely that this desire to be nonhuman and the inability to comprehend genuine relationships have been feeding into each other since Fujimine left the Mantinel Witches Association, but it's likely that she was already feeling ostracized from her peers before her revolt. In spite of this theory, however, Fujimine was portrayed with one meaningful connection—Tricaputo. A witch around Fujimine's age. Together, they formed a mentor-pupil blood contract—a magical bonding ritual between two people who wish to "become like family". It's not clear if this has always been the case, but the Mantinel Witches Association's members currently consider the contract to be a great taboo. When Desscaras announced hers with Ichi, her peers were shocked and very concerned.

Because of their proximity in age, the contract between Fujimine and Tricaputo comes across more convincingly as two teenagers running off to elope (or whatever other rash act to consummate their relationship). There was clearly a level of mutual trust and affection, because both parties had to put themselves at risk in order to form the contract. Tricaputo gave Fujimine her agency, and Fujimine put her life in Tricaputo's hands. Bear in mind that the risk of death doing go both ways—if Tricaputo dies, so does Fujimine, but Fujimine's death doesn't affect Tricaputo's life. The bottom line is that Fujimine risked everything to tie her heart to Tricaputo's.

Assuming the mentor-pupil blood contract has always been seen as taboo makes the stakes of Fujimine and Tricaputo's bond even greater. It's not far-fetched to conclude that Fujimine sought a way to express her love to Tricaputo, and in doing so (further) ostracized herself from her peers, leading to her revolt. Years later, Fujimine had no idea that Tricaputo was hiding in the Association, which suggests that Tricaputo left with her back then. Regardless of the amount of time they spent together, Tricaputo ultimately abandoned Fujimine. After giving up everything for Tricaputo, it was likely at this point that Fujimine began her spiral into the love-obsessed mass murderer she is now.

The canon lore up until this point illustrates a very twisted villain who manipulates and misuses the concept of love to drive her actions. I believe that her narrative is further enhanced by comphet, and I cannot imagine her character without it or lesbianism at her core. Also. I'm gay. So yeah.

❤Shitsuren❤

The Bleeding Heart Majik

Who is Shitsuren?

Classified as the Bleeding Heart Majik, Shitsuren is an easygoing, charismatic vagabond who loves to meet new people—especially witches. More importantly, Shitsuren is my ITW sona/OC (I kinda go between the two...).

Shitsuren claims she hasn't been alive for very long, but she's already thinking about death. Just one thing—no matter how torn up her body is, or how potently she is poisoned, she will completely recover in moments, without fail and without consequence. The healing is so fast that she often forgets entirely what it was like to take damage in the first place. This has caused her to treat her life and wellbeing with little regard, and she will often dive headfirst into dangerous situations or treat threats with a flippant curiosity.

Though she's not a human-hater, Shitsuren has no interest in aligning herself with the Mantinel Witches Association. That being said, she loves spending time with its members. This is because Shitsuren uses human life force as a source of sustenance, and a witch's vigor is the most potent and delicious (in her opinion, at least).

Additionally, Shitsuren romanticizes the human experience, but feels like she is fundamentally unable to completely understand it. She believes that this incongruence is due to her inability to die—without the inevitability of death, she will never live a life as authentic or satisfying as a human will. Unfortunately, this has led to the utterly bizarre conclusion that she must die in order to obtain the happiness that she believes only humans possess. For the record, she does not realize that death is permanent.

Trial and Abilities

Shitsuren tells her trial as follows: "A true love's kiss will do!". The kicker? She's not interested in being acquired. Although her lifestyle and penchant for dating witches seems counterintuitive to her aversion to acquisition, Shitsuren can't seem to shake her habits. Instead, she swiftly breaks off her relationships before she inevitably falls in love. Super healthy.

Shitsuren's signature ability is essentially life-steal. When the rhythm of a nearby heart reaches a high enough BPM, she is able to scoop some of its energy ("life force") and take it into herself. To the victim, getting a pinch of their energy stolen in this way feels like their heart skipping a beat or two.

To Shitsuren, humans have the most comforting and warm life force, with those with high magical potential (witches) being the most fulfilling. She claims that even a cadet's energy is "at least ten times tastier" than the average human's. That being said, most trained witches instinctively will bolster their defenses after Shitsuren attempts to take a bite. Oh well!

With the life force that she takes, Shitsuren gains a sense of fulfillment (like eating when hungry). As she takes more from hearts of the same species, her appearance will begin to resemble theirs, hence her remarkably humanoid appearance (well, barring a few things she can't seem to get rid of regardless of form).

❤FUJIREN / CHOCHU❤

aka Fujimine x Shitsuren

Shitsuren met Fujimine while on a date with a titled witch. Fujimine arrived on a personal mission to eliminate the witch, completely interrupting the date. Shitsuren was extremely shocked by Fujimine's superb killing ability and apparent bloodlust—as well as smitten with the grace Fujimine exhibited for a murderer. As infatuation blended with her desire to experience death, Shitsuren found herself overwhelmed by the feeling of butterflies in her stomach. If anyone could make her fall in love kill her, it must be Fujimine.

In addition to seeing if Fujimine can kill her, Shitsuren has also been trying to take pieces of Fujimine's life force. For some reason, it would seem that Fujimine possesses far more of it than any other witch Shitsuren has met before. Because Fujimine is interested in purging her own humanity and becoming a majik, she's interested in completing Shitsuren's trial to make it happen. But... Shitsuren doesn't want to be acquired, and Fujimine isn't looking to get her life force sapped away. Thus, these two are engaged in a toxic, two-sided game of cat and mouse.

Fujimine and Shitsuren are not in a committed relationship, but they always find their way back to the other. Neither can accept their feelings for the other in fear of the consequences that may follow. That being said, they can't stop being around each other, stuck in a will-they-won't-they game of trying to "catch" the other in a moment of vulnerability. They hopelessly gravitate around each other but are doomed to self-sabotage any and all opportunity for intimacy and true companionship. Unless...?
